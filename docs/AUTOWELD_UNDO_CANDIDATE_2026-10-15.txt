User-approved behavior
- One drag that also welds creates TWO normal native history steps.
- First Undo restores the welded-away vertex/topology, keeping the moved position.
- Second Undo restores the preceding move.
- Redo reverses that sequence. An ordinary drag without welding remains one step.
- The user withdrew the one-step grouping request; no SuperHold grouping was added.

Implementation

- A named MAXScript undo-on context covers the native polyOp.weldVerts operation.
- It runs only after existing native Hold/SuperHold has ended, while not suspended,
  disabled, restoring or redoing. Unsupported history contexts skip the weld
  without forcing Resume/EnableUndo or touching someone else's transaction.
- Rejected geometry or an exception cancels this weld's own native record.
- Last transaction states are logged; native DLLs are unchanged.
- No geometry snapshots or custom Restore implementation were added to runtime.
